About Ledgerfair

Ledgerfair is a small, independent Australian website that publishes free calculators for tradies, sole traders, contractors, landlords and home buyers.

What we do

We turn published Australian rules (tax rates, super, HECS/HELP thresholds, stamp duty tables) into calculators you can check. Each page shows the formula, a worked example and links to the official source.

What we are not

We are not a bank, broker, tax agent or financial adviser, and we are not affiliated with the ATO, SRO Victoria, ASIC or any government body. Results are general estimates, not advice. For decisions, talk to a registered tax agent or licensed adviser.

How we stay independent

The calculators are free and need no sign-up. We don't sell or share what you enter, because it never leaves your browser. If we ever add paid templates or advertising, they will be clearly labelled and will not change any calculation.
