How it's calculated

  1. Sell price = cost × (1 + markup %).
  2. Profit = sell price − cost.
  3. Margin = profit ÷ sell price × 100.
  4. Markup needed for a target margin = margin ÷ (1 − margin).
  5. Price at target margin = cost ÷ (1 − margin).
  6. Inc-GST price = ex-GST price × 1.10.

Worked example

A sparky buys $1,000 of cable and fittings (ex GST) and adds a 25% markup. The materials go on the quote at $1,250 ex GST, or $1,375 inc GST. The $250 profit is a 20% margin, not 25%. To make a 30% margin, the markup needs to be 42.9%, a price of $1,428.57 ex GST.

Why it matters

Mixing the two up is one of the easiest ways to underprice. If you need a 20% margin to cover overheads and profit, and you add a 20% markup, you're short on every job. Your overheads come out of the price, so margin is the number to check. Work out your labour rate separately with the tradie charge-out rate calculator.

FAQ

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit as a percentage of your cost. Margin is profit as a percentage of the sell price. The same job always has a lower margin than markup: a 25% markup is a 20% margin.

Is a 20% markup the same as a 20% margin?

No. A 20% markup on $1,000 of materials gives $200 profit on a $1,200 price, which is a 16.7% margin. To make a true 20% margin you need a 25% markup.

How do I convert margin to markup?

Markup = margin ÷ (1 − margin). For a 30% margin that's 0.30 ÷ 0.70 = 42.9% markup. To go the other way, margin = markup ÷ (1 + markup).

What markup should a tradie put on materials?

There's no set rule. Many trades add somewhere around 10–30% on materials to cover ordering, pick-up, storage, waste and warranty risk, but check what's normal in your trade and area. Price labour separately from your charge-out rate.

Do I calculate markup before or after GST?

Before. Work markup and margin on ex-GST cost and price. If you're registered for GST, claim the GST on your purchases as a credit and add 10% to your sell price (ATO). If you're not registered, your cost includes the GST you paid and you don't add GST.
