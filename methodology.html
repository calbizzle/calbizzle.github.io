Methodology & sources

How our calculators work, where the numbers come from, and their limits.

Principles

  • Official sources first. Rates and thresholds come from the ATO, SRO Victoria, Fair Work Ombudsman and ASIC Moneysmart.
  • Show the working. Each calculator lists its formula and a worked example you can reproduce by hand.
  • Tested. The calculation code is checked against hand-worked examples (50 automated checks) before each release.
  • Dated. Figures were last checked on 29 Oct 2026 and apply to the 2026–27 financial year (1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027).

Known simplifications

  • Tax calculators assume Australian residents for tax purposes and don't cover every offset or levy (for example the Medicare levy surcharge).
  • The stamp duty calculator covers Victoria only and excludes foreign purchaser duty, off-the-plan and pensioner concessions and registration fees.
  • The mortgage calculator uses monthly compounding; lenders calculate daily.

Primary sources

  • ATO: Tax rates for Australian residents
  • ATO: Super guarantee
  • ATO: Study and training loan repayment thresholds and rates
  • ATO: Rental expenses you can claim
  • SRO Victoria: General land transfer duty rates
  • SRO Victoria: PPR current rates
  • Fair Work Ombudsman: National Employment Standards
  • ASIC Moneysmart: Mortgage offset accounts
